# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want square_impact to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone,
whatever their experience level, background, or identity.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive
feedback gracefully, focus on what's best for the project and its users, and
own and learn from mistakes.

**Don't:** harass, insult, or demean anyone; make sexualised comments or
advances; troll; publish someone's private information without their
permission; or behave in ways that would be inappropriate in a professional
setting.

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when you're representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
privately through GitHub ([@moexx399](https://github.com/moexx399)). Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or other
contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
of the Contributor Covenant.
